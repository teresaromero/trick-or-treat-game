Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated October 24, 2026.

Short version: this game doesn't collect anything about you. It only counts page visits, anonymously. I'm Teresa Romero, the person responsible for this site.

What I collect

Anonymous page-view counts, through GoatCounter: which page was opened, the referring site, your browser, your screen size and your country. It also counts a few game actions, anonymously: a game starting or ending, pausing, the quit question, and whether a game started with keyboard play or the slower pace on. There are no cookies, no personal data and no tracking across sites, and there are no accounts or ads. I count visits because it's in my interest to know whether anyone plays the game (legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)); the counts can't be tied to a person. The game doesn't send your scores anywhere.

What your browser stores

The game saves one small value in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • trick-or-treat:best: your best score.

You can remove it at any time by clearing site data for this page.

Third parties

The pages load a small counting script from GoatCounter, which receives the visit details listed above, under GoatCounter's privacy policy. The fonts are served from this site, so no font provider sees your visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is for a general audience. It asks for no personal data, so there is nothing to collect from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this changes (for example when a shared leaderboard is added), this page will be updated with a new date before it does.

Contact

Questions, or want to ask about your data? Open an issue on GitHub. See also the Terms.
